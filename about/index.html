About

An independent software studio in Silver Spring, Maryland.

Who we are

Seven Oaks Digital, L.L.C. is a software company founded in 2026 in Silver Spring, Maryland. Mike Marshall is its Managing Member.

What we make

We make mobile games and apps for iPhone and Android, and we publish them ourselves. Our first is LEXSUM, a word-and-math game that's in testing now.

How we work

  • Small and independent

    We build our own products, start to finish: design, code, testing and support.

  • Only the data we need

    Our apps collect only what they need to work. We don't sell your data or use it to target ads.

  • Made to be read

    We design for readability, with light and dark themes and clear type.

  • Real people answer

    Email reaches the people who build our apps.
