Privacy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy covers the Seven Oaks Digital website, sevenoaks.digital, and email you send us. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking of any kind, and it has no forms or accounts. It doesn't load anything from other companies' servers.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. We don't receive or use those logs. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles them.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

  • We use it only to reply to you and to keep a record of our conversation.
  • We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone, except Google, whose Google Workspace service hosts our email.
  • We keep emails only as long as we need them. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Our apps

Each app has its own privacy policy that explains what it collects. LEXSUM's is at lexsum.app/privacy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
