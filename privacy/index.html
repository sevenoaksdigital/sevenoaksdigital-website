Privacy

How this website handles your information.

Effective November 19, 2026

Seven Oaks Digital, L.L.C. (“Seven Oaks Digital”, “we”) makes apps and runs this website. This policy covers sevenoaks.digital and messages you send us. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked from the app and its store listing. LEXSUM’s is at lexsum.app/privacy.

What we collect

  • When you visit this site: nothing directly. The site doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no forms or accounts.
  • Hosting logs: our hosting provider may automatically log technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site. We don’t use those logs to identify visitors.
  • When you contact us: your email address, name if you include it, and whatever you put in your message.

How we use it

We use your messages only to reply to you, provide support and keep a record of our conversation. We don’t sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising or use it to profile you.

Who receives it

Service providers that host this website and our email process data on our behalf and only to provide those services. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for up to 12 months after our last exchange, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we need them longer to meet a legal obligation.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. We’ll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights; see our app policies for details that apply to residents of the EU and UK.

Do Not Track

This site doesn’t track visitors over time or across other websites, so it doesn’t respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and restricted account access, to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This site isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date.
